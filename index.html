Sun Mingli

Researcher in Algorithm and AI Design

My research sits at the intersection of technology and art, investigating how design knowledge can be represented, learned, and transformed through machine computing. I am interested in the dialogue between age-old human craftsmanship and machine intelligence, particularly in the design of structures where geometry, topology, material behaviour, and embodied craftsmanship intersect. My work draws on graph learning, generative AI, and imitation learning, with a focus on human–AI interaction and the philosophical implications of computation for design practice.
