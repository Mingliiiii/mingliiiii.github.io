Sun Mingli

Researcher in Computational Design

Hi, I'm Mingli. I am a researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, structural architecture, human–AI collaboration, and construction robotics. My research focuses on design methodology at the intersection of algorithm design, neural networks, and geometry, with an emphasis on human-AI interaction theory. I am also interested in the philosophy of technology.

I have academic experience at the College of Design and Innovation and the College of Architecture and Urban Planning, Tongji University, as well as the Co-Intelligent Architecture Lab, Institute of Future Human Habitats, Tsinghua SIGS.

Email ResearchGate LinkedIn

Research Interests

Graph learning for traditional craftsmanship. Leveraging graph neural networks to capture the geometric, topological, and mechanical features of mortise-and-tenon joints, transforming experienced craftsmanship into a digital and intelligent paradigm.

Generative AI for architectural conceptualization. Examining how text-guided diffusion models and fine-tuning strategies influence early-stage spatial reasoning and design creativity in modular structural design, measured through empirical frameworks such as the Creativity Support Index.

Human–AI collaborative design. Developing imitation learning-based agents that learn from human demonstrations to enable co-intelligent design workflows for modular assembly-oriented structural tasks.

Construction robotics. Intelligent robotic systems for high-rise building façade finishing, integrating algorithm design with field deployment to improve construction safety and efficiency.
