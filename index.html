Designing intelligence for structures, tools, and the built environment.

I'm Mingli Sun, a researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, structural architecture, and human–AI collaboration.

My work explores how graph neural networks, diffusion models, and imitation learning can support generative design, modular assembly, and robotic construction.

Graph-based generative design for timber joints

Mortise-and-tenon structures · GNNs · PyTorch Geometric

Generative AI for modular structural design

Diffusion models · LoRA · Human–AI interaction

A personalized diffusion-model workflow and empirical study using the Creativity Support Index to examine how AI-generated spatial imagery shapes early-stage design reasoning.
